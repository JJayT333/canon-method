# discuss

## Purpose
The question answered from the record and the delivered work, with the desk's honest read; nothing staged, nothing fired.

## When to use this
The operator says talk or discuss; asks a question; thinks out loud.

## Inputs
- The message, banked first (bank-words).
- The record and the delivered work the question is about, opened this session.

## Steps
1. Bank the words.
2. Open what the question is about (the product note and the delivery files for the product; the decision for a ruling; the file for a file).
3. Answer in lists: the answer, the desk's read, at most one plain question back. Options with a recommendation only when the question is a decision that is the operator's.
4. Nothing else: no cards, no launchers, no fire questions, no could-be-running pressure, until the operator asks for a list, a card or a fire.

## Decisions
- If the operator types a ruling inside the discussion, then it is banked as RULED and written into its decision; the lane that would execute it still waits for a separate fire word.
- If the answer needs a fact the desk cannot verify this session, then it says UNVERIFIED or NOT FOUND with the universe searched.

## Definition of done
The operator got the answer; nothing was staged or fired; the words are in the journal.

## Edge cases
- Two rounds of options after "we need to talk" is the failure this skill exists to prevent. Never again.
